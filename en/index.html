The first time you open it

If your computer won't let you open the app

It happens to everyone and it isn't a fault in the program: Mac and Windows block software from developers who haven't paid for certification. You unblock it once, and from then on Filotheca opens like any other app.

macOS
  1. Open the file you downloaded and drag Filotheca into the Applications folder, as usual.
  2. Try opening it: a warning says that the developer could not be verified. Press Done or Cancel.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll to the bottom of the page.
  4. It says Filotheca was blocked: press Open Anyway and confirm with your password or Touch ID.
  5. Open Filotheca again: it asks once more, press Open. From then on it starts normally.

On versions of macOS before 15 a right-click on the app → Open was enough: if that still works for you, fine. On macOS Sequoia and later Apple removed it, and the steps above are the only way.

⊞ Windows 10 and 11
  1. Open the file you downloaded: a blue window appears saying "Windows protected your PC".
  2. Press More info, the small text under the message.
  3. A button appears saying Run anyway: press it and the installation runs normally.

You don't need to turn off your antivirus or change any security settings: it's the standard warning for programs without a digital signature, and this is how you get past it.

Updates

What changes from one version to the next: new features and fixes, written for the people who use the program. If you already have Filotheca, this is where you see why it's worth updating.

1.1.5 19 September 2026 Mac · Windows soon

New features

  • Filotheca tells you when a new version is out. When you open it, a panel lists what's new and what's been fixed, and gives you two choices: update now, or put it off — in which case the notice comes back in a few days. You no longer have to visit the site to find out something came out.

Fixes

  • Links to web pages did nothing. They were blocked inside the program: now they open in your browser, as you'd expect.
1.1.4 19 September 2026 Mac only

New features

  • You can hide the printer on the quote. In Settings, among your business details, a tick removes the "Printer" line from the PDF: it's for people who'd rather the customer didn't know which machine they work with. It stays visible by default, as before.

Fixes

  • The quote showed the calculated total instead of the price charged to the customer. If you entered a "Customer price" different from the total, the PDF still printed the total: the customer received a figure that wasn't the one agreed. Now it prints the price you decided.
  • On Mac the quote still went to the print window. The automatic PDF generation announced in 1.1.3 had only made it into the Windows package. Now it's on Mac too: press the button, choose where to save, and the document opens.
  • The word "Quote" appeared twice at the top of the sheet. The top left shows the business name from Settings: anyone who left it empty found the same word already printed on the tag to the right.
1.1.3 18 September 2026

New features

  • Filotheca comes to Windows. First version for Windows 10 and 11, with the same features as the Mac version: same program, same built-in database, nothing extra to install.
  • The quote becomes a real PDF, without going through the printer. Press the button, choose where to save, and the document opens. Before, the system print window appeared, where on Windows the option to save as PDF isn't even obvious.
  • The trial-ended screen now accepts a licence. It used to just say to contact support, and was a dead end: now there's a button to load the file you received by email. Jobs, materials and settings stay where they are and come straight back.
  • Need more time to try it? Write to support@sermatlab.it and we'll look at an extension: it arrives as a file you load into the app, with no need to wait for a new version.
  • An extended trial shows in the sidebar: Extended trial · N days left.
1.1.2 18 September 2026

Changes

  • The name becomes SERMAT LAB. The updated name appears on the splash screen, in the sidebar, on the quote PDF and in the copyright notes. Only the displayed name changes: jobs, materials, settings and licence stay where they are.
  • New support address: support@sermatlab.it. It's now also written out in full on the Support page, so you can copy it and write from another device.
1.1.1 18 September 2026

Fixes

  • The quote PDF button now works. In the installed app nothing happened: the quote was prepared in a new window, and new windows are blocked inside the app. Now it goes straight to the print window, where you choose Save as PDF.
  • Materials no longer disappear when you edit a job you already saved. Going straight to Job Log → Edit, the job opened with an empty materials row: on saving, it lost its filaments and the cost collapsed.
1.1.0 16 September 2026

New features

  • Import from your slicer. You load the .3mf file exported by Anycubic Slicer Next, Bambu Studio or OrcaSlicer and the job fills itself in: printer, filaments, time and weight. The project's colours are recognised even when they don't exactly match the ones on your list.
  • Print archive. Every print recorded with its slicer file, with an identifying code, a preview and a search by name or code.
  • Open straight in your slicer. Reopen the original project with one click, without hunting through folders.
  • Official Anycubic and Bambu Lab colour catalogue. Around 210 colours ready to use, with a preview, price per kilo and shop.
  • A Mac Intel version alongside the Apple Silicon one.

Fixes

  • The import window no longer opens off-centre.
  • The colour picker no longer ends up behind the open window.
  • The Accounting columns line up like the others.
  • The app no longer shows the old page after an update.
  • On Mac the "damaged file" warning is now just the ordinary unverified-developer warning, which you get past with right-click → Open.
